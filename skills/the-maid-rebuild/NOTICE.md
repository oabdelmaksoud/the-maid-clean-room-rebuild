# Authorship and inspiration

The Maid - Clean Room Rebuild (formerly Behavior Rebuild Kit) is an independently authored methodology, skill, templates, and local validation tools. No Storytold source code, skill implementation, product assets, or research corpus is incorporated. The fictional Delimited Notebook example uses original synthetic material. The MIT license covers this project's original material; linked third-party material retains its own terms.

Public Storytold repositories informed the following methodological ideas. References are pinned to the snapshots inspected on October 11, 2026:

- Behavior-to-test traceability: [WordCraft typing cases](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft/blob/2e5ecb916bd0f3280f0a04aa5718a54137bf7fbe/docs/typing-parity.md).
- Commands shared by interfaces: [PhotoCraft command registry](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/blob/c18ee8c88bb2470b951c11e8cc7147d36d345b2f/crates/engine/src/commands.rs).
- Output-based acceptance: [PhotoCraft corpus tests](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/blob/c18ee8c88bb2470b951c11e8cc7147d36d345b2f/crates/io/tests/corpus.rs).
- Resumable work and assigned ownership: [DesignCraft contributor instructions](https://github.com/storytold/designcraft/blob/ca343e8985dc4d92dd65cf77a8198723b5f8a9eb/AGENTS.md).
- Real-work gaps beyond implemented features: [GridCraft gap ledger](https://github.com/storytold/gridcraft/blob/3926d21aa9a79d272b9f71ae60d67d7d7e5bd2c2/docs/gaps.md).

Storytold policies differ by repository. These references establish inspiration, not a uniform clean-room protocol, successful product parity, endorsement, or legal certification. Our explicit reviewed research-to-implementation packet and exposure declarations are project design choices; the sampled public material does not establish that Storytold used a formal information barrier.

The public repository code was read during methodology research. This is not a claim that the toolkit's authors were unexposed to that public material. No competitor proprietary source or private research bundle was used. Future contributions that reuse third-party material must identify the exact material, applicable license, attribution, and permission basis before inclusion.
